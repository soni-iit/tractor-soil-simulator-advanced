Field data, calibration and validation

The textbook model (κ = 1) is optimistic: on the three Kim et al. (2022) plough trials it under-predicted rear slip by 45–60 %. The calibration multipliers below absorb unmeasured axle loads, tyre deformation, tillage-induced soil state and test definitions. They are specific to the tractor and soil they were fitted on.
